## 0. Root causes the critic did not see (read this first)

1. **The "flat beige sky" is not missing sky code.** `SH.skyFS` (1364-1372) already has a gradient, sun, stars, moon. Three things hide it: (a) the default art style is 0 and the shader quantises the gradient into 5 flat bands (`floor(smoothstep(..)*5.)/5.`, line 1368), which is the "dome seam" in `20_horizon.png`; (b) the overview camera (pitch 0.78, fov 0.8 rad) never looks above the horizon: the top of the frame is 0.38 rad below it, so the "sky" you see is the clear colour `col.hor` (2146) behind a 1600 m slab floating in a void; (c) the horizon colour is cream (0.9,0.86,0.78) at noon (2116), so even the horizon band reads as beige. Fix is atmosphere + a designed backdrop below the horizon, not just a nicer gradient.
2. **The diamond pattern is two bugs stacked.** (a) `terrainCol` (1489-1494) picks `grass` vs `grass2` from `hsh(i>>2,j>>2)` = hard 4x4-cell (40 m) blocks, plus a per-cell `meadow` switch at `slope>0.25`; at camera yaw 0.7 the axis-aligned blocks appear as diamonds. (b) `buildChunk` (1501) flips the triangle diagonal on a checkerboard `((i+j)&1)`, so on any curved ground neighbouring cells alternate light/dark (very visible on snow in `04_season_day260.png`). Colour is also per cell (both triangles share `s=hsh(i*7+1,j*13+5)`), never blended.
3. **The sand shoreline is baked at the wrong water level.** `buildChunk` colours gravel where `wl-6<h<wl+1.5` using the water level at build time (1497, 1503) and any cell within 330 m of the lower lake centre, so it is wrong as soon as the lake moves (the lake swings 0-20 m), is one cell wide with a 10 m saw-tooth edge, and also paints any low pocket nearby (the "rectangular sand pocket"). The pond pocket in `04_season_day140.png` / `50_rain_overview.png` is a disconnected depression inside the lake mask: `buildLake` (1580-1587) tests only `mask` and `min height < maxH`, no connectivity.
4. **Night glow:** `waterFS` body colour `wc=mix(uShallow,uDeep,..)` (1350) uses constants and the opaque `op` mix, never multiplied by light level, and `uSky` is only mixed at `fr*0.3`. Terrain goes dark because `setLight` (2109-2120) collapses `I` to 0.12 and hemi to ~0.06 with no moon.
5. **Seasons barely touch vegetation:** autumn on trees is `col*vec3(0.88,0.9,0.8)*uAutumn*0.5` (1314); `uAutumn=max(0,sin(2pi(doy-135)/360))*0.8` (2153) peaks at the autumn equinox (doy 225) and is already 0.2 in mid-summer. Snow on trees uses `smoothstep(0.3,0.8,n.y)`, but cone faces have n.y of about 0.4, so almost no white.
6. **Tree placement** (1551-1558): 70 seeded discs with linear falloff on an 11 m jittered grid = round polka-dot clumps, 2 near-identical conifers, `s=1.2+0.9*r` scale, cap 2600 each. Rocks (1525): 8-triangle pyramid, 130 instances. Grass (1527): 1 triangle per blade, 3 per tuft, fixed colour independent of the ground.
7. **Engine facts that shape every design:** terrain is 160x160 cells of 10 m, 4x4 chunks, non-indexed flat-coloured triangles, `VEX=3` vertical exaggeration; one `litFS` with `uMat` ids (0 plain, 1 terrain, 2 conifer, 3 grass, 3.2 flower, 4 solar) and instancing via attributes 5-9; water is a separate pass that samples a copy of the opaque scene; sky is a full-screen triangle; post is a single full-screen pass; 3 art styles (0 faceted default, 1 soft, 2 naturalistic); quality tiers `QUAL` (1439): shadow 0/1024/2048, particles 300/800/1500, pixel ratio 1/1.5/2. The game auto-drops quality when `frameMs>22` (2201).

## 1. Art direction (decision needed: option A recommended)

Target: a **lit miniature diorama**: matte faceted geometry, warm key light, cool hemispheric fill, atmospheric perspective, a limited palette, gentle grade. One art bible, applied through constants, not ad hoc tweaks.

- **Palette (display-space RGB):** meadow (0.47,0.61,0.28) / deep grass (0.32,0.47,0.24) / forest floor (0.19,0.29,0.16) / dry ochre (0.72,0.66,0.38) / rock warm-grey (0.57,0.54,0.49) and (0.46,0.43,0.41) / snow (0.92,0.94,0.97) / conifer (0.13,0.30,0.22) / broadleaf (0.28,0.47,0.19) / birch trunk (0.86,0.85,0.80). Saturation lower than today's lime (0.45,0.6,0.27 with sun gain pushes it neon); highest saturation reserved for water and the few signal colours (roof, pins, parcel outlines).
- **Light model:** sun colour and hemi colours driven by sun elevation through one keyframe table (`SKYK` in `p1_sky.js`): zenith / mid / horizon / glow per elevation; overcast and storm blend toward a slate ramp; night = moonlight (cool, 0.3-0.6 intensity, shadows kept) instead of a collapse to black.
- **Grade (post pass, 2385):** saturation 1.10 (dropping to ~0.7 in storms), mild S-curve, cool shadow lift, existing vignette. One uniform per knob.
- **Backdrop options (decision):** A) *diorama on a misty plain*: below-horizon rays hit a plane at slab base height, shaded as a soft cloud sea with a soft slab contact shadow, plus 2 analytic ridge silhouettes above the horizon (zero geometry, pure shader). B) infinite world (far terrain ring + fog): loses the cutaway/skirt diorama and conflicts with `buildCut` clip plane, high risk. C) neutral studio gradient: cheapest, least charming. **Recommend A.**
- **Tree scale (decision):** current trees are about 10-12 m so turbines read 10x; recommend `TREE_SCALE` 1.45 (prototyped), range 1.0-2.0.

### 3.1 Sky and light (1.1-1.7) -> WP1, WP7
- **Diagnosis:** section 0 items 1 and 4; `setLight` 2109-2120, `skyFS` 1364-1372, `frame` 2146-2152.
- **Design:** replace `setLight` by the keyframed version in `p1_sky.js` (sky table by sun elevation, overcast/storm slate blend, moon direction as a stored constant used for sky disc, night light and shadow so they agree, true sun vector kept separately from the light vector for the disc). Replace `skyFS` with `skyAll(d)` = `skyFull` (gradient, ridges, stars, moon, sun disc, clouds) above the horizon and `skyBelow` (misty plain, slab shadow) below. Share `SKYFN` GLSL between sky and water programs (string concat). Cloud style: crisp 3-tone for styles 0/1, soft for style 2 (`uSkyStyle`).
- **Parameters:** cloud plane y=4200 (above max camera y ~3400), scale 0.00032, coverage `mix(0.66,0.30,cloud)-0.12*storm`; plane y = `baseY+1`; slab half extent 800; stars 150 cells, threshold 0.93, radius 0.10-0.40 cell; sun disc `smoothstep(0.9995,0.99985)`.
- **Cost:** sky pass is 1 full-screen quad, about 12 hash-noise taps per pixel (cloud 4 octaves x 2 + below-plane 4 x 2); water FS adds one `skyAll` per water pixel. Quality gates: Low = 2 octaves, no cloud self-shadow, no water reflection of clouds; Medium = 3 octaves; High = as prototyped.
- **Steps:** (1) land `SKYFN`/`SKYU` and new `skyFS`; (2) land `setLight` + `col` table + uniform plumbing; (3) fog chunk (`applyFog`) into lit and water; (4) post grade uniforms; (5) re-enable shadows at night (`sOn` guard at 2140).
- **Risks:** `uSunDir` doubles as moon light at night (keep a separate `uSkySun` for the dome); uniforms not present in a program are `null` locations (harmless). No determinism risk.
- **Verify:** `03_overview_spring_h12` and `h06/h19/h02` cameras at hours 6, 12, 19, 1; `20_horizon`; `66_canyon_cliff`.
- Cloud shadows over terrain, god rays, sun glint: TODO in WP7 (design: second noise tap in `litFS` using the same cloud field, darkening the sun term; glint = existing spec in `waterFS` times the cloud shadow).
