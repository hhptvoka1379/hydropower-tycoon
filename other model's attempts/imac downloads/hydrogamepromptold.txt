# 1. CORE PHYSICS & FLUID DYNAMICS ENGINE (REAL MATH)

## 1.1 Hydropower Generation & Pumping
Gross Head ($H_g$) = Elevation_Upper - Elevation_Lower (meters).
Net Head ($H_n$) must account for friction:
$H_n = H_g - H_f$
Where friction head loss ($H_f$) uses Darcy-Weisbach:
$H_f = f \cdot \frac{L}{D} \cdot \frac{v^2}{2g}$
- $f$ = Darcy friction factor (~0.015 for smooth concrete/steel)
- $L$ = Penstock length (m)
- $D$ = Penstock diameter (m)
- $v$ = Flow velocity (m/s) = $Q / A_{pipe}$ where $A_{pipe} = \pi \cdot (D/2)^2$
- $g$ = 9.81 m/s²

**Turbine Power (W):** $P_{gen} = \rho \cdot g \cdot H_n \cdot Q \cdot \eta_t$
**Pump Power Required (W):** $P_{pump} = \frac{\rho \cdot g \cdot H_g \cdot Q}{\eta_p}$
- $\rho$ = 1000 kg/m³
- $\eta_t$ = Turbine efficiency (0.85-0.92)
- $\eta_p$ = Pump efficiency (0.80-0.88)

## 1.2 Reservoir Volumetrics & Mass Conservation
Reservoirs are modeled as inverted frustums (truncated pyramids) to simulate natural topography.
Volume at depth $d$: $V(d) = \frac{d}{3} \cdot (A_{top} + A_{bottom} + \sqrt{A_{top} \cdot A_{bottom}})$
Depth from Volume: Solve quadratic equation derived from frustum volume.
**Mass Conservation:** $\Delta V_{upper} = -Q_{flow} \cdot \Delta t + A_{surface} \cdot (P_{rain} - E_{evap})$
$\Delta V_{lower} = +Q_{flow} \cdot \Delta t + A_{surface} \cdot (P_{rain} - E_{evap})$

## 1.3 Equipment Wear & Cavitation (Softened for Gameplay)
Cavitation occurs when Thoma's Cavitation Parameter ($\sigma$) drops below critical $\sigma_c$:
$\sigma = \frac{H_{atm} - H_{vapor} - H_{suction}}{H_n}$
Where $H_{suction}$ is the height of turbine above tailwater. 
**Wear Accumulation Rate per tick:**
$Wear = BaseWear \cdot (\frac{P_{current}}{P_{rated}})^2 \cdot (1 + CavitationPenalty)$
Where $CavitationPenalty = 10$ if $\sigma < \sigma_c$, else $0$.
Health drops by Wear % per hour.

## 1.4 Structural Limits (Dam Raising)
When player raises dam wall, hydrostatic pressure on the dam base increases:
$P_{max} = 0.5 \cdot \rho \cdot g \cdot H_g^2$ (Force per unit width)
Construction cost must scale exponentially with $H_g^2$ to reflect real civil engineering limits.

# 2. RENEWABLE ENERGY & METEOROLOGICAL MATH

## 2.1 Wind Power Generation
Power extracted by wind turbine (W):
$P_{wind} = 0.5 \cdot \rho_{air} \cdot A_{swept} \cdot v^3 \cdot C_p \cdot \eta_{mech}$
- $\rho_{air}$ = 1.225 kg/m³
- $A_{swept} = \pi \cdot R^2$ (Blade length = R)
- $v$ = Wind speed (m/s) at hub height
- $C_p$ = Power coefficient (Betz limit = 0.593, use 0.40)
- $\eta_{mech}$ = 0.95

**Wind Shear (Height scaling):** $v_{hub} = v_{ref} \cdot (\frac{H_{hub}}{H_{ref}})^\alpha$
- $\alpha$ = 0.143 (1/7th power law for open terrain)
If $v > 25$ m/s (cut-out), $P_{wind} = 0$ (safety shutdown).

## 2.2 Solar PV Generation
DC Power output of a panel:
$P_{dc} = A_{panel} \cdot G \cdot \eta_{panel}$
- $G$ = Solar Irradiance (W/m²). Peaks at 1000 W/m² at solar noon.
- $\eta_{panel}$ = 0.20 (20% efficiency)
**Temperature Derating:** Panels lose efficiency as they heat up.
$P_{ac} = P_{dc} \cdot [1 - \gamma \cdot (T_{cell} - 25)]$
- $\gamma$ = Temperature coefficient (~0.004 / °C)
- $T_{cell} = T_{ambient} + \frac{G}{800} \cdot 25$

## 2.3 Evaporation (Penman-Monteith Simplified)
Evaporation rate (mm/day) based on temperature, wind, and solar radiation:
$E = 0.408 \cdot \Delta \cdot (R_n - G) + \gamma \cdot \frac{900}{T+273} \cdot u_2 \cdot (e_s - e_a) / (\Delta + \gamma \cdot (1 + 0.34 \cdot u_2))$
(You may simplify this to a mass-transfer model for game performance: $E = k \cdot (e_s - e_a) \cdot (1 + 0.5 \cdot u_2)$, where $e_s$ is saturation vapor pressure at temp $T$, $e_a$ is actual vapor pressure).

# 3. ECONOMIC SYSTEM & GRID SIMULATION

## 3.1 Dynamic Pricing & Duck Curve
Base Price per hour $P_{base}(h)$ follows the demand curve. 
Renewable supply impacts price dynamically (Merit Order effect):
$Price(t) = P_{base}(t) \cdot (1 - k \cdot \frac{RenewableGen(t)}{TotalDemand(t)}) + Noise(t)$
- If $Price(t) < 0$, allow negative pricing (player pays to take power - great for pumping).
- $k$ = price elasticity factor (use 1.5).

## 3.2 Financial Mathematics
**Net Present Value (NPV) for Upgrades:**
$NPV = -CapEx + \sum_{t=1}^{N} \frac{R_t - O\&M_t}{(1+r)^t}$
(Player should see projected NPV in the Build Menu to evaluate upgrades).
**Levelized Cost of Storage (LCOS):**
$LCOS = \frac{CapEx + \sum \frac{O\&M_t}{(1+r)^t}}{\sum \frac{E_{discharged}}{(1+r)^t}}$ (Track this stat for the player).

**Profit Tick (per hour):**
$Profit = (P_{gen} \cdot Price_{sell} - P_{pump} \cdot Price_{buy}) - Maintenance - LoanPayment$
